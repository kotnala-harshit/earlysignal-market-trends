How to read these results Score methodology & limitations

The first-mover opportunity score (FMOS) combines velocity (30%), cross-platform confidence (20%), geographic diffusion (10%), commercial opportunity (25%), and saturation headroom (15%). Every component is bounded from 0 to 100.

Breakout: velocity ≥75 and saturation <55. Emerging: score ≥60. Mature: saturation ≥65. Otherwise, Watch. Labels follow this order.

Source histories are normalized separately for each product, market, and source, then averaged by source when multiple markets are selected. They show relative direction, not comparable market size. Backtests require observations at both the cutoff and the exact 14-day endpoint.

These scores are research leads, not demand forecasts. Validate opportunities with customer research and commercial evidence. This dashboard is an exported snapshot; it does not collect live signals.
